How to install a new face

Pick

Find a face here in the gallery.

Install

In the clock's panel, open Relógio → Mais mostradores → Ver a galeria online (Clock → More faces → Online gallery) and tap it. Or tap Download .json here and use Instalar de um arquivo (Install from a file) in the panel.

Use

The face joins the clock's list next to the built-in ones, with no reflashing.

Requires firmware v1.6.0 or newer (download). If a face doesn't show up in the clock's online gallery yet, download its .json here and use Instalar de um arquivo.

Why not install straight from here? The clock only speaks http on your local network, and an https site can't send files to it. So installing starts from the clock's panel.
