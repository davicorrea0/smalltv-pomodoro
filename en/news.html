What's coming

Built or nearly built, coming in the next test versions. Want something else first? Tell us in the open discussion on GitHub.

Miniapps

Faces with live data that Home Assistant sends: washer, lights, plugs. Their own page in the panel, with a thumbnail and the states each one can show.

Rotation per item

Each face or miniapp stays on screen for the time you pick. A miniapp with no data skips its turn, and Home Assistant can show one right away.

Focus sessions

Sessions that end by themselves after N focuses, like Deep focus (3h15) and Gentle focus (1h15), plus your own session templates.

AI usage

Claude and Codex limits on the clock, sent by a small PC companion that only sends the numbers.

More languages

German and Russian letters in faces. Panel and clock in German, Russian and French as extra languages, downloaded only by those who pick them.

Memory in Settings

How much the firmware, files and RAM use and how much is left, explained for non-technical people.
