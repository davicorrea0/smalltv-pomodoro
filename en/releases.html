Firmware

Releases 55 releases

Every firmware release, newest first, with what changed in each one and its files. The easiest way to update is the Update now button in the clock's panel; the files here are for installing through /update or for checking.

Do not use v1.15.1 or v1.20.0. 1.15.1 made screen ghosting worse and 1.20.0 could leave the clock unable to start. If yours no longer starts, follow the recovery guide (in Portuguese).

Through /update, send the .bin.gz without unpacking it; from 1.20.1 on, send the .bin.gz.sig (the signature) with it. The uncompressed .bin is for flashing over a USB-TTL cable. SHA256SUMS.txt checks the files.
