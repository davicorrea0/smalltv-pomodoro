What it is

The clock: GeekMagic SmallTV Ultra

A small desk cube with Wi-Fi and a 240×240 colour screen (ESP8266 chip and ST7789 display). From the factory it shows the time, weather and photos, with GeekMagic's closed firmware.

The firmware: SmallTV Pomodoro

A new program for the same device, written from scratch and open on GitHub. You install it from the clock's own web page, like a regular update, and it gains new clock faces, a Pomodoro timer and the tomato.

Everything that comes with it

Weather

From Open-Meteo, no sign-up or API key. A 5-day forecast, °C or °F, and a warning when rain starts within the hour.

Focus game

Day streaks, a yearly garden, 10 achievements and a year of focus sessions kept on the clock. See the game.

Photos and GIFs

Upload them from the panel; the browser crops them to 240×240. The clock checks every GIF before showing it.

Night brightness

The screen dims from sunset to sunrise, or on a schedule you pick.

Alexa

“Alexa, turn on Pomodoro”: start, pause and stop the timer by voice on your local network, with no account or skill. How to set it up.

Web panel

On your phone or computer, in three languages. Optional password, backup, diagnostics and one-tap updates, checked and signed before the firmware is replaced.

How to install

No cable and no software on your computer: everything happens in the browser, on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

Download .bin All releases

  1. Upload it on the factory page

    Open the IP the clock shows in your browser, go to Firmware Upgrade, pick the .bin and upload it. The clock restarts by itself.

  2. Connect it to Wi-Fi

    If the clock does not join your network by itself, scan the QR on its screen with your phone and pick yours.

    If it does not connect, do not unplug the clock to try again: 3 restarts in a row open recovery mode.

  3. Open the panel

    Type the IP shown on the screen in your browser (or http://smalltv.local/), pick a face and your weather city, and start a focus session.

Installing formats the clock's file storage: photos from the factory firmware are not kept. The saved Wi-Fi stays; settings start from scratch.

Got ERROR[4]: Not Enough Space, a black screen or Wi-Fi that will not connect? The full guide covers each case, recovery mode and the status of going back to the original (on hold for now).
