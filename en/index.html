Why use it

The clock you already have, made for focus

The timer is the point

Focus, short and long breaks, a daily goal and the activity you are doing. The clock keeps a year of sessions, and a plant grows with your streak up to a golden tomato.

Installs without a cable

You upload the file on the clock's own factory web page, like a normal update. Some units need a small loader first; the guide shows how.

Checked updates

New versions arrive with one tap in the panel, signed, and the clock checks the whole file before switching. If something goes wrong, recovery mode opens with no button: power-cycle it 3 times.

13 built-in faces and 41 more in the gallery, each one a small file the panel installs without updating the firmware.

Everything else that comes with it

It runs on the clock itself. With internet, the clock gets the time and the weather (Open-Meteo), and the panel gets updates from this website. No account, no cloud.

Weather

From Open-Meteo, no sign-up or API key. A 5-day forecast, °C or °F, and a warning when rain starts within the hour.

Focus game

Day streaks, a yearly garden, 10 achievements and a day card to share. See the game.

Photos and GIFs

Upload them from the panel; the browser crops them to 240×240. The clock checks every GIF before showing it.

Night brightness

The screen dims from sunset to sunrise, or on a schedule you pick.

Alexa

“Alexa, turn on Pomodoro”: start, pause and stop the timer by voice on your local network, with no account or skill. How to set it up.

Web panel

On your phone or computer, in English, Portuguese or Spanish. Optional password, backup and restore, diagnostics, and screen colour and rotation settings.

How to install

No cable and no software on your computer: everything happens in the browser. You need a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

Download .bin All releases

  1. Download the firmware

    Get the .bin file from the button above.

  2. Upload it on the factory page

    Open the IP the clock shows in your browser, go to Firmware Upgrade, pick the file and upload it. The clock restarts by itself.

    Got “ERROR[4]: Not Enough Space”?

    The factory update space varies between units. In that case, go through a small loader first:

    1. Download smalltv-mod-loader.bin (giovi321/smalltv-mod v2.8.2) and upload it under Firmware Upgrade. The screen goes black with the loader, and that is normal: the clock is on and waiting for the next step.
    2. Join the open Wi-Fi network SmallTV-Loader from your phone or computer.
    3. Open http://192.168.4.1/update and upload the SmallTV Pomodoro .bin. Do not unplug it until the Pomodoro screen (the Wi-Fi QR code) appears. The clock restarts by itself after the upload.

  3. Connect it to Wi-Fi

    If the clock does not join your network by itself, scan the QR on its screen with your phone, confirm the SmallTV-Pomodoro network and pick yours (2.4 GHz networks only).

    If the Wi-Fi does not connect, do not unplug the clock to try again: 3 restarts in a row, each under 30 s, open recovery mode (safe, but confusing).

  4. Open the panel

    Type the IP shown on the screen in your browser (or http://smalltv.local/). Pick a face and your weather city, and start a focus session. New versions arrive with one tap in the panel.

Installing formats the clock's file storage: photos from the factory firmware are not kept. The saved Wi-Fi stays; settings start from scratch. Upload your photos again from the panel.

Pictures of every step, how to find the panel and what to do if something fails: full guide.

Going back to the original firmware

Since the 1.24 memory layout change (4M2M), going back to GeekMagic's original firmware over Wi-Fi has not been tested on a real clock yet. The step-by-step instructions are on hold until we test it with a serial cable to make sure it is safe — do not try it unless you can reflash over a cable (USB-TTL).

Questions

Will it work on my device?

Only on the GeekMagic SmallTV Ultra (ESP8266). See is this my device?

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no account and no subscription.

Is it safe?

The clock checks every update (size, CRC, full decompression and the project's signature) before switching, and the current firmware keeps running if anything fails. If it freezes, recovery mode opens with no button. Flashing any other firmware is always at your own risk.

Where does my data go?

It stays on the clock. The clock gets the time from time servers and the weather from Open-Meteo; the panel gets updates from this website. There is no project account or cloud.

What about the photos already on the clock?

Installing formats the file storage, so they are not kept. The saved Wi-Fi stays. Upload your photos again from the panel.

Can I go back to the original firmware?

The step-by-step instructions are on hold until we test going back from the 1.24 memory layout on a clock with a serial cable. Details.
